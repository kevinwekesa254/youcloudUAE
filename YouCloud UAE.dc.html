● How it works

Every till, every kitchen, every
staff shift — one live picture.

Seven things every UAE merchant does. All running on the same shared data, from a single app.

01
Bill on the floor

Scan, bill, take payment — on one device.

Every till runs POS and payment together. The same terminal that scans the barcode also settles the card, prints the receipt, and drops the sale into your books.

02
Inventory

Stock that stays right across every outlet.

Stock decrements the instant it sells — anywhere, on any channel. Snap a supplier invoice and our OCR keys it into the ledger. Low-stock alerts, inter-store transfers, batch and expiry tracking.

03
Loyalty & messaging

Customers who come back on purpose.

Every card sale, every QR scan writes to the same customer graph. Segment by spend, frequency, and product mix. Fire SMS and email offers — the way UAE actually shops.

04
Settlement & recon

The number in the dashboard is the number in the bank.

Because youcloud acquires the card and rings the sale, both events post to the same ledger. Same-day settlement (T+0) to your Emirates NBD, FAB, ADCB or Mashreq operating account.

05
Integrated back-office

HR, bookkeeping and marketing — wired into every sale.

The back-office isn't a separate stack you sync overnight. Payroll runs on the shift data your POS logged. Every sale auto-posts to your books, FTA-ready. Campaign segments read from live transactions.

06
AI-native support

An AI that knows your business — because it's your data.

Because every event lives on one shared graph, insights aren't a report you download. Store cameras see what's happening on the floor. Automations act on live signals. Forecasts train on your own transactions.
